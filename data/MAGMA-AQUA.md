# Magma VS Aqua

Battle-only set. Rebuild the roster with `node scripts/build-magma-aqua.js` and validate it with `node tools/test-magma-aqua.cjs`.

The roster pairs 98 Magma and 98 Aqua evolution-line groups and adds 79 Dark/Poison groups (groups may overlap). It includes 559 Pokémon/forms, the requested Mega whitelist, the explicitly requested legendary forms, and Ditto. Regional pre-evolutions are retained. Latias/Latios Megas follow the all-forms request; Mega Rayquaza is the explicit chase exception.

Eligible Pokémon have a total 10% trainer chance. Dual-team Pokémon split that chance equally. Eligible Mega trainer rolls upgrade to Marc/Adrian 10% of the time. Groudon/Kyogre trainer rolls always use their respective leader, including Primal forms; unowned versions also exist. Team trainers constrain Z/Tera types; unowned versions use all 18 types. Sprites within a trainer pool do not constitute separate versions.

If a team trainer claims a Pokémon whose primary type belongs to the opposing faction and which has Dark/Poison typing, its native printed type is forced to that Dark/Poison type. This includes Marc/Adrian versions. Examples: Magma's Tentacruel is Poison, Magma's Sharpedo is Dark, Aqua's Incineroar is Dark, and Aqua's Glimmora is Poison. Team-specific Z/Tera types still apply to those treatments. These cards receive an additive 50 P bonus labelled “Woher habt ihr das??”, after multipliers. Pokémon without Dark/Poison typing and same-team cards retain existing typing and receive no bonus.

The opening order is two Commons, one Common/Uncommon (50/50), one Uncommon, one Energy/Energy Foil, then the Rare-or-better final slot. The final slot uses Rare/Super Rare 70/30. Species are uniform within a tier except Primal Groudon/Kyogre, which have 0.001 species weight relative to other Super Rares in every main-card pool, including God Packs. This makes their plain versions more valuable than all untreated Megas and their leader versions more valuable than every other non-Full-Art trainer version. The existing inverse-pullrate formula provides the higher payout automatically. Energy Foils keep uniform species weights. Normal eligible Pokémon use normal/Z/Tera weights 70/15/15. Native printed types are uniform. Eligible Full Arts have 10% chance. Draws are independent and can repeat a species. Exact pullrates and chase odds are recalculated for this collation.

The bonus slot has a 15% Energy Foil Pokémon chance, with equal species weights. God Packs remain 1%: four Rare/Super Rare slots (65/35), a guaranteed Full Art Mega final slot and a guaranteed Rare Energy Foil. The final slot can instead receive one of the reserved jackpots.

Mega Rayquaza exists only as a Dragon Full Art Mega Super Rare, with no Energy Foil or trainer version. The next rarest versions are Marc's Full Art Primal Groudon (Ground/Fire) and Adrian's Full Art Primal Kyogre (Water). Each leader Primal version is assigned one tenth of the smallest ordinary version's per-pack probability; Mega Rayquaza gets one hundredth. This deliberately makes them extremely rare and preserves their ranking as the roster changes. The exact pullrate table is calculated by the engine, including God Packs and the probability of at least one copy across independent slots.

Battle groups base pullrates by Pokémon/form and Full Art status. All native types, trainer names, Z, Tera and Energy Foil versions share that group's chance of at least one card per pack. The engine aggregates slot probabilities before computing the union, rather than summing overlapping per-version appearance chances. Existing treatment/rarity multipliers and fixed bonuses then apply to the individual card. Generation weights and exact-version collection identities remain unchanged.

Base prices use `round(10 / sqrt(group probability))`, clamped to rarity bands: Common 100–199 P, Uncommon 300–499 P, Rare 700–1,499 P, Super Rare 2,200–99,999 P, Special Rare 150,000–250,000 P. Primal Groudon/Kyogre retain a 5,000 P minimum base price to preserve their premium ranking after variants are grouped. These bands guarantee higher tiers beat lower tiers with equivalent treatments. Packs and scores stay in memory; this set does not write to collections or Supabase.

Ditto is a Super Rare with exactly one plain Normal-type version: no trainer, Z, Tera, Full Art or Energy Foil. It has a fixed total payout of 2,500 P, displayed as Ditto-Bonus, with no additional base or rarity payout. Its unique fake-out uses an eligible lower-value Rare decoy, followed by a pink morph and a jiggle reveal. Reduced-motion mode and the show-all action bypass the animation.
